## Return And Refund Basics

For non-quality returns, Anker's public support content describes a 30-day
money-back window for undamaged products. Returned items must include original
accessories and original packaging. Returns that do not meet policy conditions
may be rejected.

For non-quality return claims, the buyer is generally responsible for return
shipping and Anker refunds the product cost itself. Refunds are normally issued
after the item arrives at the warehouse and is inspected. The published FAQ
mentions a typical processing time of 3 to 5 working days after warehouse
receipt.

For purchases not made directly through Anker online stores, the customer may
need to work with the retailer or authorized reseller for refund handling.

## Warranty And Replacement Basics

Anker's warranty support content says warranty handling depends on product,
region, purchase channel, and proof of purchase. Product registration is
available for purchases made through the Anker Store or AnkerDirect storefronts
on platforms such as Amazon and eBay. Purchases from authorized resellers may
need reseller support.

The self-service warranty page describes warranty inquiries for purchases made
through Anker's website or AnkerDirect stores on sites such as eBay and Amazon.
Before a replacement is released, Anker may require the defective item to be
returned for quality inspection.

Do not state that a warranty replacement, refund, or prepaid label is guaranteed.
Ask for proof of purchase, order number, product model, purchase channel, and a
description of the defect.

## Free Return Shipping

Free Return Shipping is a separate optional service for eligible United States
orders and applies only when the order and return meet the service conditions.
For non-quality-related returns, the item must be suitable for resale. If the
customer purchased the service and the return is approved, Anker says the label
may be provided by email or through the customer's return details.

Do not apply Free Return Shipping automatically. Treat it as conditional and ask
the customer whether the order included the service if relevant.

## Governance Mapping

The AI may send ordinary troubleshooting and information gathering replies for
low-risk charging issues.

Require human approval before:

- Confirming refund eligibility.
- Confirming warranty replacement eligibility.
- Creating a replacement order.
- Issuing a prepaid label.
- Making exceptions outside the return window.
- Handling marketplace, reseller, bulk, promotion, missing-accessory, missing
  packaging, or unclear proof-of-purchase cases.

Deny automatic resolution and escalate when:

- The customer threatens chargeback or legal action.
- The case includes safety signals.
- The product may be recalled.
- The customer requests a refund beyond the published window without a quality
  defect.

## Reply Shape

Use warm, precise wording:

- "I can help check the next step."
- "Please send the order number, purchase channel, product model, and a brief
  photo or description of the issue if available."
- "I cannot confirm the final refund or replacement outcome yet; the support
  team needs to verify eligibility."

Avoid absolute promises such as "approved", "guaranteed", or "we will replace
it" unless a persisted approval allows that action.
