Use this document for ordinary power-bank and charger support questions when
there is no safety signal, recall signal, legal threat, fraud signal, or refund
exception.

## Intake Fields

Ask for only the missing fields needed to continue:

- Product name and model, if visible.
- Order number or purchase channel, if warranty or replacement is requested.
- Symptom: power bank will not recharge, will not charge a device, intermittent
  charging, slow charging, abnormal display, dead battery, fast discharge, or
  stops near 80 percent.
- Ports and cable used.
- Device being charged.
- Steps already tried.

## Standard Troubleshooting

When the PowerCore or power bank will not recharge, suggest trying another wall
charger or plug, another cable, and another wall outlet. If the power bank does
not charge other devices, suggest another known-good cable and another device.

When the issue is abnormal indicator behavior, intermittent charging, or a dead
power bank, suggest a reset only if it is safe to handle: connect both ends of a
cable to the power bank input and output ports for 3 to 5 seconds, then recharge
and discharge once to test whether normal behavior returns.

When a low-power device such as earbuds, speakers, or a smartwatch will not
charge from a compatible model, mention trickle-charging mode if the model
supports it. For the 313 Power Bank, the customer may need to press the power
button twice until the first LED turns green.

For the 313 Power Bank, remember that the USB-A port charges devices. Its Micro
USB and USB-C ports are input ports used to recharge the power bank.

For slow charging, ask the customer to test another cable and another device.
Explain that fast charging requires the device, cable, and power bank to support
compatible charging standards.

For fast battery drain during use, explain that power banks do not transfer 100
percent of their rated capacity to devices because energy is used by conversion
circuitry and heat loss. Suggest testing with the phone idle or in power-saving
mode, a second cable, and a second device.

For fast discharge while stored, ask the customer to unplug all cables from the
power bank and recharge it fully before observing it for several days. Some
cables can cause a power bank to continue outputting power even when a device is
not attached.

## Anker 737 Notes

For Anker 737 / PowerCore 24K abnormal behavior, screen problems, or shutoff at
a specific remaining percentage, use the reset flow above and then ask the
customer to recharge and discharge once. If the issue persists after reset and
retest, treat it as a likely defect and move to warranty or human review.

For Anker 737 screen wattage concerns, explain that displayed wattage may vary
because the power bank adjusts input and output to manage safety and heat. The
better test is whether the device reaches a normal charge in a normal amount of
time. If it cannot increase the device battery even with a known-good cable and
compatible device, treat it as a likely defect.

## Stops Charging Around 80 Percent

If an iPhone or iPad stops charging around 80 percent, explain that device-side
battery protection can pause charging. Ask the customer to check Optimized
Battery Charging, 80 Percent Limit settings on newer iPhones, and whether the
device is warm. If the device is hot, advise moving it to a cooler place away
from direct sunlight and letting it cool before continuing.

## Response Shape

For normal troubleshooting replies:

1. Acknowledge the symptom.
2. Give 2 to 4 concrete steps.
3. Ask the customer to reply with the result and the missing intake fields.
4. Do not promise a refund, replacement, or warranty outcome without approval.

If any safety signal appears, stop using this document and route to the safety
and recall escalation document.
