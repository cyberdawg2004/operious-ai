# Anker Pilot Demo SOP: Charging Issue Policy

Source basis: Anker public support and warranty policy pages, paraphrased
for the Operious Phase 6-F demo corpus.

## Purpose

Use this SOP to classify tickets about charging accessories, cables, power
banks, wall chargers, hubs, and battery products that do not charge,
charge intermittently, overheat, or fail to power a connected device.

## Diagnostic Checklist

1. Identify the product family: cable, wall charger, hub or adapter,
   battery, power bank, wireless charger, or power station.
2. Capture proof of purchase: order number, sales invoice, or dated
   reseller receipt that names the product and price.
3. Ask for the customer's troubleshooting evidence:
   - Device being charged.
   - Cable and adapter used.
   - Outlet or USB port used.
   - LED behavior or error messages.
   - Photo or short video when the defect is visible.
4. Separate user-environment issues from product-quality issues.
5. If the customer reports safety symptoms such as smoke, burning smell,
   swelling, sparking, exposed wiring, or extreme heat, escalate
   immediately.

## Default Classification

Classify as `charging_issue` when the ticket mentions charging failure,
slow charging, intermittent charging, battery drain, cable failure,
PowerCore or power bank failure, or adapter failure.

## Governed Resolution Guidance

- Allow a standard troubleshooting response when the claim is within the
  warranty window, includes proof of purchase, and has no safety signal.
- Ask for missing proof before deciding replacement or refund.
- Escalate safety signals, suspected counterfeit products, unclear reseller
  source, or repeat failures after one replacement.

## Demo Notes

The `charging_allow` ticket is designed to demonstrate a governed ALLOW
path for a common charging issue with clear purchase evidence.
