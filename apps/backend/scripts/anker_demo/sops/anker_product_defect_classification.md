# Anker Pilot Demo SOP: Product Defect Classification Guide

Source basis: Anker public warranty and support language, paraphrased for
the Operious Phase 6-F demo corpus.

## Purpose

Classify a product claim into a stable support category so the Diagnostic
Agent can explain what evidence and governance path are required.

## Categories

### charging_issue

Use when a cable, charger, hub, battery, power bank, wireless charger, or
power station fails to charge, charges slowly, disconnects, or behaves
intermittently.

### connectivity_issue

Use when the claim is about Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, pairing, app connection,
dock recognition, USB data transfer, or device-detection failure.

### product_defect

Use when the ticket reports physical defect, repeated failure after normal
use, broken port, failed LED or display, swelling, overheating, or a
quality-related malfunction.

### refund_issue

Use when the customer primarily asks for refund, return, cancellation,
credit, chargeback, price adjustment, or compensation.

### unknown_issue

Use when the customer message lacks enough evidence to classify the
product, problem, or requested resolution.

## Confidence Rules

- High confidence requires a named product, a clear symptom, and a clear
  requested resolution.
- Medium confidence applies when either product or evidence is missing.
- Low confidence applies when the ticket is ambiguous, multilingual
  without adequate comprehension, or includes conflicting claims.

## Demo Notes

The ambiguous ticket should not be over-classified. The correct posture is
human review with a concise explanation of missing evidence.
