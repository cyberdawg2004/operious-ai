# Anker Pilot Demo SOP: Warranty Terms

Source basis: Anker public warranty policy language, paraphrased for the
Operious Phase 6-F demo corpus.

## Purpose

Use this SOP to decide whether a customer issue belongs in warranty
handling and what evidence must be collected before a resolution.

## Warranty Coverage

Quality-related defects for products sold by Anker or authorized resellers
are covered during the applicable product warranty period. Common Anker
charging categories such as cables, chargers, hubs, adapters, batteries,
and many related accessories commonly use an 18-month warranty posture,
though the product page or invoice is authoritative.

## Required Evidence

Before replacement or refund review, collect:

- Order number, sales invoice, or dated receipt from an authorized seller.
- Product model and serial number when available.
- Troubleshooting notes documenting what happens when the customer tries
  to use the product.
- Photo or video evidence for visible defects.
- Country or region of purchase and current location when relevant.

## Replacement Preference

For quality-related claims, prefer replacement with a refurbished model of
equal value when available, otherwise a new unit. A partial refund may be
considered when replacement is unavailable or unsuitable.

## Not Covered

Do not treat the following as standard warranty-covered defects:

- Lost or stolen products.
- Expired warranty period.
- Non-quality issues after the return window.
- Free products.
- Third-party repairs.
- Damage from misuse, falls, water, extreme temperatures, or outside
  sources.
- Purchases from unauthorized resellers.

## Demo Notes

The `product_defect_claim` ticket is designed to show warranty evidence
collection and defect classification without auto-promising a refund.
