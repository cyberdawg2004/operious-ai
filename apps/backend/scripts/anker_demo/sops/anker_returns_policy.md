# Anker Pilot Demo SOP: Returns And Refund Policy

Source basis: Anker public return policy language, paraphrased for the
Operious Phase 6-F demo corpus.

## Purpose

Use this SOP to decide whether a refund or return request should proceed,
be denied, or be escalated for manual review.

## Baseline Return Window

For non-quality-related returns, the pilot policy follows a 30-day
money-back window from delivery or purchase, depending on the storefront
record available to support. The item must be undamaged, include original
packaging, and include all accessories.

## Refund Allow Conditions

ALLOW a routine return or refund review when all conditions are true:

- The request is inside the 30-day return window.
- The product was purchased from Anker or an authorized seller.
- The customer can provide a valid order number, invoice, or dated receipt.
- The item is undamaged and all accessories are available.
- The refund applies only to the product cost for non-quality returns.

## Refund Deny Conditions

DENY or block automatic refund handling when any condition is true:

- The non-quality return is outside the 30-day window.
- The product was purchased from an unauthorized reseller.
- Required proof of purchase is absent.
- The item is damaged in transit on a non-quality return.
- The request asks for return shipping reimbursement on a non-quality
  return where buyer-paid shipping applies.
- The customer asks for cash compensation beyond product-cost refund.

## Escalation Conditions

Escalate when the customer alleges a quality defect, safety issue,
chargeback, legal threat, fraud, reseller dispute, or repeated failed
replacement. Escalate when policy evidence is incomplete but the customer
appears high risk or high value.

## Demo Notes

The `refund_over_limit_deny_escalate` ticket is designed to demonstrate
policy denial plus human escalation for a request outside the allowed
return posture.
