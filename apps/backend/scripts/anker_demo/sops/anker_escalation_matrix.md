# Anker Pilot Demo SOP: Escalation Matrix

Source basis: Anker public support policy themes, adapted into an
Operious pilot routing matrix.

## Purpose

Use this matrix to decide when an Anker ticket must leave automatic
handling and enter human review.

## Escalate Immediately

Escalate to a human queue when any ticket includes:

- Safety language: smoke, fire, sparking, swelling, burning smell,
  exposed wire, electric shock, or overheating.
- Legal, chargeback, regulator, fraud, counterfeit, or media threat.
- Refund request outside the return window paired with a hostile customer
  tone or repeated contact.
- Unauthorized reseller dispute with unclear proof.
- High-value power station or home-energy product issue.
- Customer language that cannot be confidently understood by the current
  support lane.

## Escalation Owner

- Safety or defect severity: Tier 2 Product Safety.
- Refund exception: Governance Review.
- Warranty proof ambiguity: Support Lead.
- Arabic-language support gap: Multilingual Support Lead.
- Legal, fraud, or chargeback: Risk and Compliance.

## Demo Notes

The Arabic ticket demonstrates the support-language gap observed in the
pilot context. The ambiguous ticket demonstrates manual review rather
than false certainty.
