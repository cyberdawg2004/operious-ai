# Jiao Ma Intro

Subject: Anker support pilot for the Arabic-language gap

Hi Jiao,

I hope you have been well. Since our Transcosmos Indonesia days, I have
kept thinking about the places where Anker support teams were doing the
right work but the tooling was not meeting the real operational need.

One gap stood out: Arabic-language customers could reach the queue, but
the workflow did not reliably preserve language context through
triage, policy decisions, and the final customer reply. I built Operious
to solve that exact class of problem: multilingual ticket intake,
governed diagnostics, manager-visible approvals, and a Command Center
where the team can inspect every decision before anything touches a
customer.

I would like to propose a focused Anker pilot: $8K for 60 days, using a
small set of support scenarios first, then expanding only if the results
are useful to your team.

I can share the Command Center live and walk you through the Arabic
ticket flow, the governance trace, and the operator view in 20 minutes.
